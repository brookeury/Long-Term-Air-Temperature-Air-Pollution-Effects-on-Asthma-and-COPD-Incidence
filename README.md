# Long-Term-Air-Temperature-Air-Pollution-Effects-on-Asthma-and-COPD-Incidence

This repository contains the code to replicate the analyses performed in "Impacts of Long-Term Air Temperature and Air Pollution on Asthma and COPD Incidence in the German National Cohort" (currently under review).

Data Availability: The datasets used in this study are not publicly available. Access to the German National Cohort (NAKO) data can be requested from https://transfer.nako.de/.

Support Statement: This project was conducted with data (Application No. NAKO-1159) from the German National Cohort (NAKO) (www.nako.de). The NAKO is funded by the Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space (BMFTR) [project funding reference numbers: 01ER1301A/B/C, 01ER1511D, 01ER1801A/B/C/D and 01ER2301A/B/C], Federal States of Germany and the Helmholtz Association, the participating universities and the institutes of the Leibniz Association.

For more details, please refer to the presentation slides from the International NAKO (German National Cohort) Conference 2026: 
Ury B, Schikowski T, Karrasch S, Nikolaou N, Kress S, Nagrani R, Peters A, Schneider A, Wolf, K. Impacts of Long-Term Air Temperature and Air Pollution on Asthma and COPD Incidence in the German National Cohort. doi: https://doi.org/10.82296/hmgu-nefeli.939p8-0tz24
